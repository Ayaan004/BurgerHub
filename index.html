OUR STORY

Founded in 2009, Burger Hub started as a small food truck in downtown New York with a simple mission — serve the freshest, most delicious burgers in the city. What began as a passion project quickly grew into a beloved brand.

Today, we operate across 25+ locations nationwide, staying true to our roots. Every patty is hand-pressed, every bun is freshly baked, and every meal is crafted with love. We source our ingredients from local farms to ensure quality in every bite.
